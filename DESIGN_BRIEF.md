# Design brief — Optica Medicală Florești

- **Tone:** premium optical editorial; precise, warm, unhurried.
- **Palette:** warm paper `#F4F0E8`, ink `#111513`, optical blue `#1857FF`, soft sage `#C9D5C7`, signal coral `#F05A47`.
- **Typography:** Libre Bodoni for display, DM Sans for body and interface text.
- **Hero:** an interactive focus lens that sharpens an eye chart and follows the pointer; static centered state under reduced motion.
- **Signature detail:** optical measurement lines and a face-shape/frame-fit story that turns a repeated review theme into a visual idea.

No prices, email, WhatsApp number, brand list, or claims beyond the Google listing and reviews are invented. Missing details live in `src/data/business.ts` and are replaced after the owner meeting.
